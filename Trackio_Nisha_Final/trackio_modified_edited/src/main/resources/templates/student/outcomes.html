📘 How attainment is calculated

  • Each project you tag to a CO counts as 25% attainment toward that CO (4 = 100%)
  • COs feed into POs by the mappings your teacher set (high/medium/low weights)
  • Strong (≥75%) means you've demonstrated that outcome convincingly
  • Emerging (<40%) means you need more projects in that area
  • This report mirrors the format used in BAETE/NBA accreditation submissions
