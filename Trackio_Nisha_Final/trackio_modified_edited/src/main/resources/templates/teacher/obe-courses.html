📘 What is Outcome-Based Education (OBE)?

OBE is the framework used by accreditation bodies (BAETE / NBA / ABET) where every graduate must demonstrably attain 12 standardized Program Outcomes (POs). POs are achieved through Course Outcomes (COs) — what students learn in each course. Students provide evidence (projects, assignments, internships) that they've attained each CO.

This system computes PO attainment automatically from the evidence tagged by students, giving you a real-time accreditation-ready report.
